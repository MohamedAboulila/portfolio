1,000 leads in five days, in a product nobody can buy online

Most lead generation advice assumes the sale closes on a screen. At Faisal Securities the close was always a conversation, and that changes what marketing is actually for.

01 / The setup

No function, no pipeline, and a product you cannot check out.

Faisal Securities is the licensed brokerage arm of Faisal Islamic Bank of Egypt. When I joined as marketing manager, there was no marketing function at all. No measurement, no pipeline. And the product had a catch: nobody opens a trading account online. Every prospect requests a call or fills a form, and a human closes. I reported directly to the Managing Director and owned digital, brand, web and traditional media.

02 / The insight

The handoff is part of the funnel.

In a high-trust category, a form fill is not a result. Ad engagement means nothing if the lead quality, the tracking and the sales follow-up are not wired together. A qualified lead in the hands of a prepared salesperson, that is the result. So the first thing I built was not a campaign. It was the measurement.

03 / Measure first

Nothing scaled before it could be read.

Before a single pound scaled, I set up GA4, Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity, so traffic, ads and on-site behavior could be read in one place. Only then did the paid campaigns on Meta and YouTube go to work, with TV media partnerships adding reach and credibility on top.

The play

01Build the measurement firstGA4, Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity, before any spend scales.
02Run paid where the attention isLead generation pipelines on Meta and YouTube.
03Borrow trustTV media partnerships for awareness in a regulated market.
04Close the loopTie every campaign to a booked call or a submitted form, then sales follow-up.
04 / The result

Demand generation doing its actual job.

One thousand leads in five days. Trading account openings up 10%. In a category where the final yes happens in a conversation, that is what the funnel is for. Not clicks, not forms. Qualified people reaching someone who can say yes.

What I would tell a founder or head of growth

If a human closes your deals, marketing's job does not end at the form fill. It ends when a qualified lead reaches a prepared salesperson, and your metrics should describe that whole path, not stop at the cheap part.

Steal this play

Map your funnel one step past the form. Write down what the sales team receives, how fast they get it, and what they say first. Fix that before you raise budget.
