How I raised ROAS from 13.56 to 19.21 while cutting ad spend by 18%

Most advice says spend more on what works. On the P&G accounts, the better question was what we were paying for that did not.

01 / The setup

Serious spend, small margins for error.

Through Publicis Groupe, I managed Amazon advertising for P&G accounts. Monthly spend reached EGP 708K, inside a campaign scope selling more than EGP 11M a month. At that size a small percentage swing is real money, so every pound of ad cost had to justify itself.

02 / The ratio, not the budget

Two ways to raise ROAS.

ROAS is revenue divided by ad spend. You can raise it by growing revenue or by shrinking cost. Teams under pressure chase the first, because it feels like progress. The second is often faster and safer, because the spend you remove was producing little of the revenue anyway.

03 / Watch the pair

ROAS and ACOS, together, every week.

Watch only ROAS and you can starve campaigns that are actually profitable. Watch only ACOS and you can feed campaigns that merely look busy. I reviewed campaigns against both, never against spend volume alone, and let the pair show where the next pound should go.

The play

01Rank the spendCompare what each campaign costs with what it returns.
02Cut the weak spotsTrim the placements and terms where the ad cost share was creeping up.
03Move budget to what earnsShift budget toward the targets returning more revenue per ad pound.
04Report it plainlyShare results and recommendations with stakeholders, with cost beside revenue.
04 / The result

More return, smaller cost share.

ROAS rose from 13.56 to 19.21, about 42%. ACOS fell from 7.37% to 5.21%, about 29%. Across the engagement, monthly spend came down 18% while revenue grew 16%. Spend and revenue are not linked one to one: some of what a budget buys is simply expensive.

What I would tell a founder or head of growth

Know your ratio by campaign. Cut waste before you scale, so the next pound works harder. Change things in steps and measure after each one. And never show a revenue number without the spend beside it.

Steal this play

Pick one efficiency pair for your own spend, like ROAS and ACOS, and review it on one fixed day every week. Never judge a week by spend volume alone.
