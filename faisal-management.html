Making a new marketing function visible to leadership

A clear account of the function’s remit helped management see how digital acquisition, brand, web, social, and traditional media fit together.

01 / The challenge

Several important workstreams needed one accountable owner.

When Mohamed joined, Faisal Securities had no established marketing function. Digital activity, brand materials, website work, lead generation, social strategy, and traditional media needed a shared direction and a clear explanation for leadership.

02 / Define the remit

Show how the parts supported the business.

He documented the scope across PPC, SEO, website redesign, lead-generation pipelines, social strategy, brand materials, and TV media partnerships. The remit connected visibility and demand generation to the brokerage’s human-led account-opening process.

03 / Make the work legible

Prepare a report, then a branded management deck.

Mohamed created an Arabic Word report describing the marketing function, then prepared a presentation in Faisal’s visual identity at management’s request. He presented the work to upper management and wrote a LinkedIn job description that also documented the role’s scope.

04 / The outcome

A documented operating remit for marketing.

The report, presentation, and role description gave stakeholders a concise view of what the new function covered and how its work connected across acquisition, digital experience, brand, and media. The broader role also carried the reported campaign results of 1,000 leads in five days and a 10% increase in trading account openings, covered in the marketing-function case study.
