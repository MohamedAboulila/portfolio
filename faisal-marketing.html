Building a brokerage’s marketing function

Creating a measurable lead-generation system for a regulated financial service where opening a trading account required a human sales conversation.

01 / The challenge

Trust mattered, and conversion needed a person.

Faisal Securities is the licensed brokerage arm of Faisal Islamic Bank of Egypt. In a regulated, trust-led industry, prospects could not open accounts online; they had to request a call or submit a form. Marketing therefore needed to generate qualified leads and connect campaigns to a human sales process.

When Mohamed joined as Marketing Manager, there was no established marketing function. He reported directly to the Managing Director and owned digital, brand, web, and traditional media.

02 / The approach

Build the operating foundation and the acquisition path.

Mohamed developed lead-generation pipelines and ran paid campaigns across Meta and YouTube. He configured Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, and Microsoft Clarity to establish measurement across traffic, advertising, and website behavior. He also pursued TV media partnerships to broaden awareness and credibility.

03 / The result

Demand generation connected to a real sales journey.

The campaign period produced 1,000 leads in five days and trading account openings increased by 10%. The measurement setup helped connect paid acquisition to on-site behavior and the next step in the sales process.

04 / The takeaway

In high-trust categories, the handoff is part of the funnel.

For a product that requires human consultation, marketing performance depends on more than ad engagement. Lead capture, tracking, follow-up, and the credibility of the institution all shape conversion.
