Building a brokerage’s marketing function

No function, no measurement, no pipeline, and a product you cannot buy online. A regulated brokerage where every account still opens through a human conversation.

01 / The situation

Trust mattered, and conversion needed a person.

Faisal Securities is the licensed brokerage arm of Faisal Islamic Bank of Egypt. When I joined, there was no established marketing function, and the product had a catch: nobody can open a trading account online. Every prospect has to request a call or submit a form, and a human closes. Marketing’s job was to generate qualified leads and connect campaigns to that human sales process.

I reported directly to the Managing Director and owned digital, brand, web and traditional media.

02 / The insight

The handoff is part of the funnel.

In high-trust categories, the handoff is part of the funnel. Ad engagement means little if the lead quality, the tracking, and the sales follow-up are not wired together. So the first build was not a campaign. It was the measurement: GA4, Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity, so traffic, advertising and on-site behavior could be read in one place before a single pound scaled.

The play

01Lead pipelinesBuilt lead-generation pipelines and ran paid campaigns on Meta and YouTube.
02MeasurementConfigured GA4, Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity across traffic, ads and on-site behavior.
03Wider reachPursued TV media partnerships to broaden awareness and credibility.
04Real conversionConnected every campaign to a booked call or a submitted form, then sales follow-up.
03 / The result

Demand generation doing its actual job.

1,000 leads in five days. Trading account openings up 10%. In a category where the final yes happens in a conversation, that is demand generation doing its actual job.

The lesson

If a human closes your deals, marketing’s job does not end at the form fill. It ends when a qualified lead reaches a prepared salesperson, and your metrics should describe that whole path.

Steal this play

Map your funnel one step past the form. Write down what the sales team receives, how fast, and what they say first. Fix that before you raise budget.
