A bit about me

01 / WHO I AM

I’m a growth marketer based in Cairo with 6+ years across startups, agencies and brands. My job is finding the constraint, the one thing actually limiting your growth, and fixing it. Sometimes that is a channel. Sometimes it is a message, a funnel step, or a dashboard nobody trusts yet.

I started in agency work, then moved into growth at Camlist, a Y Combinator-backed marketplace, where I worked across acquisition, activation and retention.

Later, as a freelancer with Publicis Groupe, I managed Amazon and Delivery Hero campaigns for P&G. With monthly ad spend reaching EGP 708K, I improved ROAS from 13.56 to 19.21 while reducing spend by 18% and growing revenue by 16%.

At Faisal Securities I built the marketing function from the ground up as Marketing Manager, reporting to the Managing Director. I developed lead pipelines and measurement, ran paid media, and rebuilt the website around the customer journey and SEO. The system produced 1,000 leads in five days and a 10% rise in trading account openings.

Today at Hands of Hope I manage paid channels, build dashboards and optimize lead acquisition. I work in Arabic and English, hold a B.Com in Business Administration from Mansoura University, and share what I learn on my blog, Marketing brainstorms.
