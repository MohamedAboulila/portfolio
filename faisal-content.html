Case study 03 · Faisal Securities

From content audit to platform-specific video growth

A Facebook content audit identified the formats earning reach. Those findings informed a 24-reel campaign built around the different ways audiences discover video across five platforms.

01 / The problem

Reach varied by platform and post format.

The brokerage published market commentary, educational content, and company news, but performance varied. Facebook and Instagram were also receiving similar creative despite differences in how audiences find and engage with content. The team needed to understand what earned attention before scaling production.

02 / The Facebook audit

Native video consistently outperformed link cards.

Mohamed audited Facebook posts by format and topic, comparing native video with link-card posts covering similar subjects. In the reviewed content, native video earned more reach across topics, indicating that format was a stronger reach driver than subject alone.

The finding became a production guideline: lead with native video, and use link cards when directing people to a link is the purpose of the post.

03 / The system

Plan the campaign around platform behavior.

Mohamed designed a staggered series of 24 reels for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube Shorts. He prepared a strategy document breaking down platform algorithms and audience behavior, then organized production and publishing in a weekly calendar with posting times tuned to Egyptian and Middle Eastern audiences. He also identified cross-posting Facebook-native creative as a reason Instagram lagged and adapted the creative approach by platform.

04 / The result

Organic video grew 200%.

Organic video grew 200% over the reported campaign period. The audit and platform strategy gave the team a clearer basis for choosing formats, adapting creative, and planning publication across channels.

05 / What this demonstrates

Content operations can be both structured and native.

The campaign paired a clear production cadence with deliberate differences in how content was distributed across channels.
