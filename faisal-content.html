From content audit to platform-specific video growth

A content audit on Facebook surfaced one uncomfortable truth, and it became a 24-reel campaign across five platforms.

01 / The situation

Reach varied, and nobody knew why.

The brokerage published market commentary, education and company news, but reach was unpredictable. Facebook and Instagram were getting near-identical creative, as if the two platforms rewarded the same things. They do not.

02 / The insight

Format, not topic, was the reach driver.

I audited Facebook posts by format and topic, comparing native video against link-card posts covering similar subjects. Native video earned more reach across topics. Format, not subject, was the reach driver. One line became the production rule: lead with native video, and use link cards only when the link itself is the point.

The play

0124-reel seriesDesigned a staggered series of 24 reels for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts.
02Platform logicWrote a strategy document breaking down each platform’s algorithm and audience behavior.
03Regional timingOrganized production and publishing into a weekly calendar tuned to Egyptian and Middle Eastern audiences.
04Native per platformFound that cross-posting Facebook-native creative was holding Instagram back, and adapted the creative approach by platform.
03 / The result

Same brand, different format discipline.

Organic video grew 200% over the reported campaign period. Same brand, same topics, different format discipline. The audit and platform strategy gave the team a clear basis for choosing formats, adapting creative and planning publication across channels.

The lesson

Every platform has its own physics. The same idea needs a different shape on TikTok than on LinkedIn, and the audit that tells you which shape is usually one afternoon of honest counting.

Steal this play

Before your next content sprint, sort your last 30 posts by format, not topic. The pattern you find is your production guideline for the next quarter.
