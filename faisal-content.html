One video idea, five platform strategies

A 24-reel campaign designed for each platform’s distribution logic instead of treating every social channel as the same feed.

01 / The problem

Cross-posting was flattening the content.

The team was publishing Facebook-native creative to Instagram, even though the platforms reward different viewing and interaction patterns. The content plan needed to support consistent output while respecting the way audiences discover short-form video on each platform.

02 / The system

Plan the campaign around platform behavior.

Mohamed designed a staggered series of 24 reels for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube Shorts. He prepared a strategy document breaking down platform algorithms and audience behavior, then organized production and publishing in a weekly calendar with posting times tuned to Egyptian and Middle Eastern audiences.

03 / The result

Organic video grew 200%.

Organic video performance grew by 200%. The work also surfaced a practical lesson: a repeatable campaign still needs platform-specific creative and publishing choices. Posting the same asset everywhere can undermine the strengths of each channel.

04 / What this demonstrates

Content operations can be both structured and native.

The campaign paired a clear production cadence with deliberate differences in how content was distributed across channels.
