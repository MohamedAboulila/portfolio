01 / The role

Work across the whole lifecycle.

From September 2022 to December 2024 I worked as a Growth Marketing Specialist at Camlist, a Y Combinator-backed marketplace, across marketing, acquisition, activation and retention. During that time I contributed to growth that took users from 30k to 150k over 18 months, and revenue from 100k to over 1M in the same period.

The play

01Behavior to decisionsAnalyzed user behavior and campaign performance, and wrote reports that turned complex findings into clear decisions.
02ExperimentationRan A/B tests to improve conversion and user experience, and tracked the key metrics behind them.
03SystemsBuilt spreadsheets and data models, and streamlined business processes with automation tools that reduced operating costs.
04Market radarResearched potential markets and products, and wrote marketing, advertising and website copy.

03 / The growth system

Acquisition, product insight, and retention worked together.

The role connected marketing analysis with user behavior, product improvements and retention inside one marketplace business. Camlist has since closed; the foundation it gave me has not.

The lesson

Growth is a system, not a channel. Acquisition, activation, retention and product insight either learn from each other weekly or drift apart expensively.

Steal this play

Build a weekly learning loop: one behavior insight, one experiment, one decision written down. In a year you will have fifty documented lessons most competitors never bothered to collect.
