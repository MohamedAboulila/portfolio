01 / Role context

Work across the marketplace lifecycle.

Mohamed worked full time as a Growth Marketing Specialist at Camlist, a Y Combinator-backed marketplace, from September 2022 to December 2024. His responsibilities spanned marketing, acquisition, activation, and retention.

02 / Growth practice

Use customer behavior to inform product and marketing.

He analyzed user behavior and campaign performance, prepared reports that translated complex findings into clear written and graphical summaries, and shared insights to inform strategic decisions and product improvements. He also researched potential markets and products and wrote marketing, advertising, and website copy.

03 / Systems and experimentation

Build a learning loop into execution.

The role included A/B testing to improve conversion and user experience, tracking key metrics, developing spreadsheets and data models, and streamlining business processes with automation tools. Automation tools streamlined business processes and reduced operating costs.

04 / The growth system

Acquisition, product insight, and retention worked together.

The role connected marketing analysis with user behavior, product improvements, and retention activity inside a marketplace business. Camlist has since closed, but the experience remains a meaningful part of Mohamed’s growth marketing foundation.
