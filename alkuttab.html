Researching growth opportunities for an education platform

A side project combining market research, channel planning, product collaboration, and retention thinking for an online education platform.

01 / Context

Connect the market picture to practical growth choices.

During this side project, Mohamed worked as a Senior Growth Marketing Specialist with Alkuttab.io, an education platform. The work covered a broad digital marketing plan and cross-functional alignment with the Product team.

02 / Research

Understand the audience and the alternatives.

He researched industry trends, customer behavior, and the competitive landscape to inform strategic decisions and refine the value proposition. He also looked for new acquisition channels and considered how market trends and user feedback could guide improvements to the website and app.

03 / Execution and measurement

Plan across channels and customer stages.

The plan covered social media, paid search, search marketing, and organic search. Mohamed tracked campaign and user-behavior KPIs, supported content optimization for visibility and engagement, and developed retention strategies intended to increase engagement and reduce churn.

04 / Takeaway

Growth planning benefits from a shared product and marketing view.

Acquisition channels, platform experience, and retention are connected. The project paired market research and channel planning with product feedback and lifecycle considerations.
