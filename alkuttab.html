Researching growth opportunities for an education platform

A three-month side project turning market, audience and competitor research into channel, product and retention moves.

01 / The situation

Growth choices grounded in evidence.

Alkuttab.io, an online education platform, needed growth choices grounded in evidence, not instinct. As Senior Growth Marketing Specialist on this side project, I paired a broad digital marketing plan with day-to-day alignment alongside the Product team.

The play

01Three lensesResearched industry trends, customer behavior and the competitive landscape, and used the findings to refine the value proposition.
02Channel planTurned the research into an acquisition plan across social, PPC, SEM and SEO, with a hunt for new channels.
03Product feedbackFed market trends and user feedback back to Product as improvement candidates for the website and app.
04RetentionBuilt engagement and churn strategy into the plan, with KPI tracking and SEO content optimization.
The lesson

Research only earns its keep when it ends in two lists: the channels you will run, and the product changes you will ask for. Everything else is trivia.

Steal this play

Before touching a channel, run the three-lens scan: market, customer, competitor. Timebox it to two weeks and force it to end in a channel plan and a product change list.
