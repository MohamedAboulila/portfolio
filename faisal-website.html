Redesigning the Arabic web experience around the full funnel

A right-to-left website structure, Arabic page content, and a funnel framework designed to help prospects understand the offer and take the next step.

01 / The brief

Make the website easier to navigate and act on.

The brokerage needed an Arabic website with a clear information structure, an experience suited to right-to-left reading, and pathways that support a considered financial-services decision. Account opening depended on a request or conversation with a representative, so the website needed to make those next steps understandable.

02 / The build

Start with the information architecture.

Mohamed confirmed a sitemap and defined a repeatable page structure: navigation, breadcrumb, hero, main content, differentiators band, and CTA bar. He developed Arabic copy for every page and considered user experience and search visibility throughout the redesign.

The positioning used verified differentiators supplied for the project: the parent bank’s 40+ year heritage, experienced account officers working in an advisory model, and concierge onboarding in which a representative visits the client.

03 / Launch

Bring the proposition into the feed.

A five-post launch campaign introduced the rebuilt website in Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى). The captions stayed with verified claims: the parent bank’s heritage, experienced account officers, and concierge onboarding. They made clear that prospective clients book a call or submit a form; accounts cannot be opened online.

Mohamed also prepared two Arabic right-to-left Word documents: a campaign brief for approval and an IT edits document listing website changes.

04 / Funnel decisions

Connect each stage to a real next step.

The six-stage framework—Awareness, Interest, Consideration, Conversion, Onboarding, and Retention—gave content, advertising, and the website one shared structure. Conversion reflected the actual client journey: request a call or submit a form, followed by concierge onboarding in which a representative visits with the paperwork.

Two strategic questions remained open: whether gated financial research (البحوث المالية) should be tracked as its own sub-funnel, and whether TikTok’s audience fits the brokerage’s core investor base.

05 / The outcome

A shared digital foundation and launch plan.

The work brought together an agreed sitemap, a reusable Arabic page system, UX- and SEO-aware copy, a full-funnel framework, and a five-post launch campaign with approval and IT documentation.

Design principles

  • Right-to-left Arabic reading flow
  • Consistent navigation and page hierarchy
  • Clear differentiators and next actions
  • Search-friendly content structure
  • Concierge sales journey in place of online account opening
