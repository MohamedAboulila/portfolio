Redesigning the Arabic web experience around the full funnel

Right-to-left structure, Arabic copy on every page, and a six-stage funnel that ends the way the business actually works: a representative visits your home.

01 / The situation

Make the next step obvious.

The brokerage needed an Arabic website with a clear structure and a right-to-left experience that respects how people actually read and decide. And because accounts open through a request or a conversation, the site had one job: make that next step obvious and easy to take.

The play

01ArchitectureConfirmed the sitemap and one repeatable page structure: navigation, breadcrumb, hero, main content, differentiators band, CTA bar.
02Arabic copyWrote the copy for every page, with UX and search visibility in every draft.
03Verified claimsBuilt the messaging on the three substantiated differentiators: the parent bank’s 40+ year heritage, account officers with 7+ years in an advisory model, and concierge onboarding.
04LaunchShipped a five-post campaign in Modern Standard Arabic, plus a campaign brief for approval and an IT edits document.

02 / The honest bits

Two questions left open, on purpose.

Whether gated financial research should be tracked as its own sub-funnel, and whether TikTok’s audience fits the brokerage’s core investor base. A funnel document that pretends to have every answer is usually hiding the interesting ones.

03 / The outcome

A shared digital foundation and launch plan.

The work brought together an agreed sitemap, a reusable Arabic page system, UX- and SEO-aware copy, a full-funnel framework, and a five-post launch campaign with approval and IT documentation.

The lesson

A funnel framework is only useful if its conversion stage matches reality. If the real next step is a phone call and a home visit, that is your conversion stage, whatever the template says.

Steal this play

Write your funnel stages from the customer’s actual next step, not from your org chart. Then check every page: does it move someone one stage, or is it decoration?

Design principles

  • Right-to-left Arabic reading flow
  • Consistent navigation and page hierarchy
  • Clear differentiators and next actions
  • Search-friendly content structure
