Improving Amazon advertising efficiency

01 / The brief

Make paid marketplace activity work harder.

Through Publicis Groupe, Mohamed managed Amazon advertising for P&G accounts. The task was to improve campaign efficiency: generate more sales relative to ad spend and reduce the advertising cost share.

Mohamed managed P&G campaigns across Amazon and Delivery Hero, overseeing monthly ad spend up to EGP 708K and monthly sales above EGP 11M. On Amazon, ROAS rose from 13.56 to 19.21 while ACOS fell from 7.37% to 5.21%.

02 / The work

Optimize against both return and cost.

Campaign management focused on performance outcomes rather than spend alone. ROAS tracked revenue generated per advertising pound, while ACOS showed what proportion of attributed sales went back into advertising. Keeping both measures in view made the efficiency change legible.

Mohamed also acted as the client point of contact, preparing performance reports and recommendations for P&G stakeholders.

03 / The outcome

More return, lower cost share.

ROAS rose from 13.56 to 19.21, approximately 42%. ACOS fell from 7.37% to 5.21%, approximately 29%. Together, those indicators show that the accounts earned more revenue per ad pound while using a smaller share of sales to fund advertising.

04 / What this demonstrates

Performance reporting tied to commercial outcomes.

This engagement combined hands-on marketplace campaign management, efficiency analysis, clear stakeholder reporting, and practical recommendations for a major consumer-goods portfolio.
