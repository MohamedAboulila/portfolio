Case study 01 · Publicis Groupe / P&G

Improving Amazon advertising efficiency

Four months, one freelance engagement, one job: make P&G’s Amazon advertising earn more per pound and cost less per sale.

01 / The situation

Serious spend, small margins for error.

Through Publicis Groupe, I managed Amazon advertising for P&G accounts. The accounts were already spending seriously, up to EGP 708K a month, inside a campaign scope selling more than EGP 11M a month. At that scale, efficiency is the whole game: a small percentage swing is real money.

02 / The insight

Watch the pair, not the number.

Most advertisers watch one number. Watch only ROAS and you can starve campaigns that are actually profitable. Watch only ACOS and you can feed campaigns that merely look busy. The fix is boring and powerful: track both, together, every week, and let the pair tell you where the next pound goes.

The play

01Both lensesReviewed campaigns against return and cost, never spend volume alone.
02Budget shiftsMoved budget toward the targets earning more revenue per ad pound.
03Cost controlCut the placements and terms where the ad cost share was creeping up.
04Clear reportingActed as the client point of contact, with performance reports and recommendations for P&G stakeholders.
03 / The result

More return, smaller cost share.

ROAS rose from 13.56 to 19.21, roughly a 42% increase. ACOS fell from 7.37% to 5.21%, roughly a 29% decrease. Across the engagement, monthly spend came down by 18% while revenue grew by 16%. More return, smaller cost share. That is the whole brief, delivered.

The lesson

ROAS and ACOS are two ends of the same seesaw. Optimizing one without the other is how accounts look healthy right up until the finance team asks questions.

Steal this play

Pick one efficiency pair for your own spend, like ROAS and ACOS, and review it on one fixed day every week. Never judge a week by spend volume alone.
